Title: Fix avatar receiver registration ownership and expose cleared segment ends

Closing DataStreamAudioReceiver currently leaves its byte-stream handler and process-wide clear-buffer dispatch entry registered. Recreating the receiver on the same Room fails, and equal sender identities in different rooms share a dispatch key.

Register and unregister each receiver on its own Room, matching the existing one-handler-per-topic constraint. Register the byte-stream handler first so a second receiver cannot replace the first receiver's RPC before the duplicate topic is rejected. Record successful ownership so closing a receiver whose start failed cannot remove another receiver's handlers.

AudioSegmentEnd.interrupted exposes the existing cleared-reader flag. Both normal and cleared readers continue to produce an end marker, allowing consumers to distinguish their completion semantics without accessing private receiver state.
